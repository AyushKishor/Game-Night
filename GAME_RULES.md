# Game Night — Rules

_Generated from the game modules by `npm run rules:generate`. The same rules are shown in the app._

24 games are fully playable. Games marked **bots** can be filled with computer players.

## Party games

### Trivia Night

_Quick-fire multiple choice. Fast and right wins._ · 1–12 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Answer the most questions correctly — quickly.

1. A question and four answers appear on the big screen.
2. Everyone picks an answer on their own phone before time runs out.
3. Correct answers score 10, plus up to 5 bonus points for speed.
4. Team mode splits the room into two teams whose points are pooled.
5. Difficulty chooses easier or harder questions.

**Scoring:** 10 points per correct answer plus a speed bonus of up to 5.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the last question wins (in team mode, the whole team shares the result); ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Emoji Movies

_Guess the film or phrase from a string of emoji._ · 1–12 players · 10–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Decode the emoji before everyone else.

1. A row of emoji appears on the big screen — it spells out a film title or a common phrase.
2. Pick the right answer from four options on your phone.
3. Right answers score 10 plus a speed bonus.
4. Play solo or turn on team mode to pool points.
5. Difficulty: Easy is films only, Hard mixes in phrases.

**Scoring:** 10 points per correct answer plus a speed bonus of up to 5.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the last puzzle wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Most Likely To

_Who in the room is most likely to…? Vote and find out._ · 3–12 players · 10–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Vote with the crowd — and try not to be the answer.

1. A “Who is most likely to…” prompt appears on the big screen.
2. Everyone secretly votes for a player (you can vote for yourself).
3. Votes are revealed. The most-voted player is the group's pick.
4. Turn on the Spicy house rule for grown-up prompts, and Sip mode for a drinking-game twist: the group's pick takes a sip.

**Scoring:** 1 point if you voted for the player the group picked.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of prompts wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Spicy prompts (adults) — Adds cheeky prompts about dating and nights out.; Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Clue Rush

_Describe the word without saying the forbidden ones. Teams race the clock._ · 4–12 players · 15–30 min

**Goal:** Get your team to guess as many words as possible.

1. Players split into two teams by seat. Teams take turns; the clue-giver rotates.
2. Only the clue-giver's phone shows the word, plus a few forbidden words.
3. Describe the word out loud without saying it or any forbidden word. Your team shouts guesses.
4. Tap “Got it” when they get it, or “Skip” to move on. The other team sees the card too and can hit “Buzz” if you slip up.
5. Play it as charades instead if you like: no talking, only acting!

**Scoring:** +1 for each word your team gets, −1 for each buzz. Skips are free.

**Ending & ties:** After every player's turns, the higher-scoring team wins (everyone on it shares the win); ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Majority Rules

_Pick a side. Score by agreeing with the crowd._ · 3–12 players · 5–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Think like the group.

1. A question with two options appears on the big screen.
2. Everyone secretly picks an option on their phone.
3. When everyone has voted (or time runs out) the votes are revealed.
4. Everyone on the bigger side scores a point. A tie scores nothing.

**Scoring:** 1 point for being in the majority.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of questions wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Quick Categories

_A category, a letter, a ticking clock._ · 2–12 players · 10–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Come up with answers nobody else thinks of.

1. A category and a starting letter appear — e.g. “Fruits” and “B”.
2. Type one answer that fits and starts with the letter before time runs out.
3. Then everyone reviews the answers. Tap any you think don't fit.
4. An answer rejected by more than half of the other players scores nothing.

**Scoring:** A valid answer nobody else gave: 2 points. A valid answer someone else also gave: 1 point.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of rounds wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Caption Clash

_Write the funniest caption. Vote anonymously._ · 3–12 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Write the captions that get the most votes.

1. A silly scenario appears on the big screen.
2. Everyone writes a caption for it on their phone.
3. All captions are shown without names. Vote for your favourite (not your own).
4. Authors are revealed with the votes.

**Scoring:** 1 point per vote your caption receives, plus 1 bonus point for the top caption (shared on a tie).

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of rounds wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Rank It

_One player ranks five things. Everyone else reads their mind._ · 3–10 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Predict how your friends think.

1. Each round, one player is the Ranker. A topic and five items appear, like “Best pizza toppings”.
2. The Ranker secretly puts the five items in order from best to worst.
3. Everyone else tries to guess the Ranker's exact order.
4. The Ranker's order is revealed and points are handed out. The Ranker role rotates each round.

**Scoring:** Guessers score 1 point per item in the right position. The Ranker scores 1 point for each guesser who got at least 3 right.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of rounds wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Secret Signal

_Find the player who shares your secret symbol — one word at a time._ · 4–12 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Find your secret partner without giving your symbol away to everyone.

1. Each round, players are secretly paired up — partners share a hidden symbol, like “Volcano”. With an odd number of players, one person is the Loner with a symbol nobody shares.
2. Everyone writes a one-word clue about their symbol (you can't use the symbol's name).
3. All clues are shown together. Pick the player you think is your partner.
4. The Loner tries to blend in and gets points if someone picks them.

**Scoring:** Find your partner: +2 (+1 bonus if you both find each other). The Loner gets +1 for every player who picks them.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of rounds wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

## Card games

### Crazy Eights

_Match the suit or rank. Eights are wild._ · 2–7 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Be the first to get rid of all your cards.

1. Everyone gets 5 cards (7 with two players). One card starts the discard pile.
2. On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's suit or rank.
3. Eights are wild: play one any time and choose the next suit.
4. Can't or don't want to play? Draw one card. You may then play a legal card or pass.
5. When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is reshuffled (the top card stays).

**Scoring:** The player who goes out scores the cards left in everyone else's hands: eights 50, face cards 10, aces 1, other cards their number.

**Ending & ties:** The game ends after the set number of rounds or when someone reaches the target score. Highest total wins; equal totals share the win.

### Go Fish

_Ask for ranks, collect sets of four._ · 2–6 players · 10–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Collect the most books (all four cards of a rank).

1. Everyone gets 7 cards (5 with four or more players). The rest form the pond.
2. On your turn, ask another player for a rank you already hold.
3. If they have any, they hand them all over and you go again.
4. If not — Go Fish! Draw one card. If it's the rank you asked for, go again; otherwise play passes on.
5. Complete sets of four are laid down automatically. If your hand is empty, you draw a card at the start of your turn.

**Scoring:** One point per book.

**Ending & ties:** The game ends when all 13 books are made. Most books wins; ties share the win.

### Old Maid

_Pair up and pass on the odd queen._ · 2–8 players · 5–10 min · bots

**Goal:** Don't be the one left holding the Old Maid (the unpaired Queen of Spades).

1. The Queen of Clubs is removed, so the Queen of Spades has no partner. All other cards are dealt out.
2. Any pairs of the same rank are discarded automatically.
3. On your turn, pick a face-down card from the next player who still has cards.
4. If it pairs with one of yours, the pair is discarded. Then play passes on.
5. Run out of cards and you're safe.

**Scoring:** Everyone who gets out scores 1 point. The Old Maid scores 0.

**Ending & ties:** The game ends when only one player has cards left. All safe players share the win.

### War

_Flip together. Highest card takes them all._ · 2–4 players · 5–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Win the most cards.

1. The whole deck is dealt face down into everyone's piles.
2. Each battle, everyone taps Flip to turn over their top card.
3. The highest card (aces high) wins every flipped card.
4. If the top cards tie, it's war: the tied players put up to three cards face down, then flip again. The winner takes the whole pot.
5. Players with no cards are out.

**Scoring:** Your score is the number of cards in your pile at the end.

**Ending & ties:** The game ends when one player has every card or after the battle limit. Most cards wins; ties share the win.

### Snap

_Spot two matching ranks and hit Snap first._ · 2–8 players · 5–10 min · bots

**Goal:** End up with the most cards.

1. Everyone gets an equal face-down pile.
2. Take turns flipping your top card onto the centre pile.
3. When the top two centre cards have the same rank, anyone can hit SNAP. The fastest snap wins the whole centre pile.
4. Snap when there's no match and one of your cards goes to the bottom of the centre pile.
5. Too slow? If the pile already changed, your snap simply doesn't count.

**Scoring:** Your score is the number of cards in your pile at the end.

**Ending & ties:** Ends when one player has all the cards or the flip limit is reached. Most cards wins; ties share the win.

### Memory Match

_Flip two, remember everything._ · 1–8 players · 5–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Find the most matching pairs.

1. Cards are laid out face down in a grid.
2. On your turn, flip two cards. A pair is the same rank and colour — like 7♥ and 7♦.
3. Find a pair and you keep it and go again.
4. No match? Everyone gets a look, then the cards flip back and the next player goes.
5. Difficulty sets the grid size: 16, 24 or 36 cards.

**Scoring:** One point per pair.

**Ending & ties:** The game ends when every pair is found. Most pairs wins; ties share the win.

### Hearts

_Dodge the hearts and the Queen of Spades._ · 4–4 players · 20–40 min · bots

**Goal:** Take as few penalty points as possible.

1. Four players get 13 cards each. Add bots if you have fewer than four people.
2. Before each round, pass three cards: left, then right, then across, then no pass.
3. Whoever has the 2♣ leads it. Everyone must follow the led suit if they can.
4. The highest card of the led suit wins the trick; the winner leads next.
5. You can't lead hearts until a heart has been played (unless you only have hearts).
6. Point cards can't be played on the very first trick unless you have nothing else.

**Scoring:** Each heart is 1 point and the Queen of Spades is 13. Take all 26 (“shoot the moon”) and everyone else gets 26 instead.

**Ending & ties:** The game ends after the set number of rounds or when anyone reaches the limit. Lowest total wins; equal totals share the win.

### Spades

_Bid your tricks with a partner. Spades are always trump._ · 4–4 players · 25–45 min · bots

**Goal:** Win exactly the number of tricks your side bids — or more.

1. Four players get 13 cards. Partners sit opposite each other (turn off team mode to play every-player-for-themselves).
2. Starting left of the dealer, everyone bids how many tricks they expect to win (0 means “nil”).
3. Follow the led suit if you can. If you can't, you may play a spade (trump) or any card.
4. The highest spade wins the trick, otherwise the highest card of the led suit.
5. Spades can't be led until one has been played on another suit (unless you only have spades).

**Scoring:** Make your side's combined bid: +10 per bid trick and +1 per extra trick (a “bag”). Miss it: −10 per bid trick. Every 10 bags costs 100. Nil bids score +100 if you take no tricks, −100 if you do.

**Ending & ties:** The game ends when a side reaches the target score or after the set number of rounds. Highest score wins; ties share the win.

### President

_Climb from Scum to President by shedding cards first._ · 3–7 players · 15–30 min · bots

**Goal:** Get rid of your cards first to become President.

1. All cards are dealt. Card order from low to high: 3, 4 … K, A, 2.
2. The leader plays one card, or a pair, triple or four of a kind.
3. Each next player must play the same number of cards of a higher rank — or pass.
4. Once you pass you're out until the pile is cleared. When everyone else has passed, the pile clears and the last player to play leads again.
5. Players drop out as their hands empty. Titles go from President down to Scum.
6. Next round, the Scum gives their best card to the President, gets back the President's worst, and leads.

**Scoring:** Each round, first out scores one less than the number of players, second out one less again, and so on down to 0.

**Ending & ties:** Highest total after the set number of rounds wins; ties share the win.

### Bluff

_Claim the right rank — truthfully or not._ · 3–8 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Be the first to get rid of all your cards.

1. All cards are dealt. The required rank starts at Ace and goes up by one each turn (A, 2, 3 … K, then A again).
2. On your turn, play 1–4 cards face down and claim they're all the required rank. You may lie!
3. Everyone else then gets a few seconds to call “Bluff!” or let it go.
4. If someone calls: the cards are revealed. If any card isn't the claimed rank, the player who played them picks up the whole pile. If they were honest, the caller picks it up.
5. If your last cards survive the challenge window, you win.

**Scoring:** Your score is the number of cards you're left holding — lower is better.

**Ending & ties:** The first player to empty their hand wins; everyone else is ranked by cards left (equal counts share a place).

### Sevens

_Build the suits out from the sevens._ · 3–8 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Be the first to play all your cards.

1. All cards are dealt. Whoever holds the 7♦ plays it first.
2. On your turn, play one card: any 7 starts that suit's row, or play the next card up or down on an existing row (6 or 8 next to a 7, and so on).
3. Rows run from Ace (low end) to King (high end).
4. If you can play, you must. If you can't, you pass.

**Scoring:** The player who goes out scores one point for every card still held by the other players.

**Ending & ties:** Ends after the set number of rounds. Highest total wins; ties share the win.

### Golf

_Six cards, lowest score wins. Match columns to cancel them._ · 2–6 players · 15–30 min · bots

**Goal:** Have the lowest total after all the holes.

1. Everyone gets six face-down cards in two rows of three, then turns up any two.
2. On your turn, draw the top card of the deck or take the top discard.
3. Swap it with any card in your grid (the old card goes face up on the discard pile)…
4. …or, if you drew from the deck, discard it and turn up one of your face-down cards.
5. When someone has all six cards face up, everyone else gets one final turn, then all cards are revealed.

**Scoring:** Ace 1, Two −2, 3–10 face value, Jack/Queen 10, King 0. Two cards of the same rank in a column score 0.

**Ending & ties:** After the set number of holes the lowest total wins; ties share the win.

### Higher or Lower

_Will the next card be higher or lower? Everyone guesses at once._ · 1–12 players · 5–10 min · bots

**Goal:** Predict the most cards correctly.

1. A card is shown on the table.
2. Everyone secretly picks Higher or Lower for the next card (aces are high).
3. When everyone has locked in — or time runs out — the next card is revealed.
4. If the next card has the same rank, nobody scores that round.

**Scoring:** One point per correct guess.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of cards wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Twenty-One

_Get closer to 21 than the dealer — just for points._ · 1–7 players · 10–15 min · bots

**Goal:** Beat the dealer's total without going over 21.

1. Everyone gets two face-up cards. The dealer gets one face-up and one face-down card.
2. Number cards count their number, face cards 10, aces 1 or 11.
3. Everyone plays at the same time: Hit to take a card, Stand to stop. Over 21 is bust.
4. When everyone has stood or bust, the dealer reveals and draws until reaching at least 17.
5. There's no betting — you just score points.

**Scoring:** Two-card 21 that the dealer doesn't match: 3 points. Beat the dealer: 2. Tie: 1. Lose or bust: 0.

**Ending & ties:** Most points after the set number of rounds wins; ties share the win.

**House rules:** Sip mode (adults only) — The screen calls out who takes a sip each round — any drink works. Please drink responsibly.

### Switch

_Shedding with action cards: pick-up twos, skips and reverses._ · 2–7 players · 10–20 min · bots

**Goal:** Be the first to get rid of all your cards.

1. Everyone gets 7 cards (5 with five or more players). Match the top card's suit or rank.
2. Aces are wild: play one on anything and choose the next suit.
3. Twos: the next player must draw 2 — unless they play a Two, which passes on 4, and so on.
4. Eights: the next player misses their turn.
5. Jacks: reverse the direction of play (no effect with two players).
6. If you can't play, draw one card, then play a legal card or pass.

**Scoring:** The player who goes out scores the cards left in other hands: aces 50, twos/eights/jacks 20, queens and kings 10, others their number.

**Ending & ties:** Ends after the set number of rounds or when someone reaches the target score. Highest total wins; ties share the win.

## Game Night leaderboard

After every finished game, each player earns 1 point for every player they finished ahead of, plus 2 points for a win (shared wins count). Points carry across every game played in the same room. Games ended early by the host award nothing.
